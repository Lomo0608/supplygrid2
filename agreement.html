Supplier Agreement

Last updated: June 2026

This Supplier Agreement applies to every account that lists inventory on SupplyGrid. By accepting it you agree to the following on behalf of your company. It works alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Right to sell

You confirm that you have the legal right to sell every component you list, that the inventory is yours to offer or that you are authorized to broker it, and that listing it does not breach any agreement, allocation, or export restriction that applies to you.

2. Accuracy of listings

You agree that the part numbers, manufacturers, quantities, date codes, and condition you enter are accurate to the best of your knowledge, and that you will keep your listings current, removing or updating inventory you can no longer supply.

3. No counterfeit or mislabeled parts

You will not knowingly list counterfeit, cloned, relabeled, salvaged-and-misrepresented, or stolen components. You understand that SupplyGrid does not inspect, test, or authenticate parts, that verification and certification badges reflect checks on your business and its certifications rather than on any individual part, and that buyers transact at their own risk.

4. Accountability and disputes

You agree to respond to buyer disputes in good faith and in a timely manner. You understand that disputes filed against your account, and how you resolve them, are recorded by SupplyGrid and may be displayed to buyers as part of your track record. Repeated unresolved disputes, or evidence of counterfeit trafficking, may result in suspension or removal.

5. Anonymous listings

If you list anonymously, SupplyGrid will not display your identity to buyers and will route inquiries through the platform. You remain fully responsible under this agreement for anonymously listed inventory, and anonymity cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is required by law.

6. Conduct

You will not manipulate ratings, demand signals, or search results, will not scrape or bulk-export platform data without permission, and will not use SupplyGrid to harass other users or to circumvent export controls or sanctions applicable to the components you trade.

7. Suspension and changes

SupplyGrid may remove listings or suspend accounts that violate this agreement or the law. We may update this agreement as the platform evolves and will post the current version here. Continued listing after a change means you accept the updated agreement.

Questions about this agreement can be sent through the contact options published on the platform.
