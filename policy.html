Anti-counterfeiting and trust policy

SupplyGrid is committed to maintaining a marketplace where buyers can trust what they source.
Last updated: June 2026
Our commitment

Counterfeiting and fraud in the electronic components industry endanger national security, aerospace and defense systems, medical devices, and industrial equipment. SupplyGrid takes this seriously. Every supplier on the platform must agree to and comply with this policy as a condition of membership.

What SupplyGrid prohibits
The following are grounds for immediate removal
  • Selling, brokering, or representing counterfeit, fraudulent, or misrepresented electronic components
  • Listing parts as stocked when they are not physically in possession or under confirmed purchase order
  • Providing false certification documentation or misrepresenting certifications held
  • Engaging in any form of online fraud, identity misrepresentation, or deceptive trade practices
  • Attempting to circumvent the platform's trust and verification systems
  • Misrepresenting the manufacturer, date code, condition, or specification of any listed component

SupplyGrid does not take possession of parts, process payments, or guarantee the quality of any transaction. Members bear responsibility for their own due diligence when sourcing components.

Supplier quality indicators

SupplyGrid uses several indicators to help buyers assess supplier credibility. None of these are guarantees, but together they provide meaningful signals.

  • Stocked vs brokered — Suppliers must accurately declare whether they physically hold inventory or are brokering it. This is the single most important disclosure on the platform.
  • Verified Business badge — Requires submission of business license, registered address, tax ID, and two independent trade references.
  • Certified Supplier badge — Requires Verified Business status plus documented quality certifications such as ISO 9001, AS9120, AS6081, AS6171, or IDEA-STD-1010.
  • Authorized Supplier designation — Indicates the supplier has stated authorization from a specific manufacturer. SupplyGrid notes this as declared by the supplier.
  • 5-criteria buyer ratings — Covers responsiveness, quoting, listing accuracy, delivery quality, and return handling. Submitted by verified buyers after transactions.
  • Trade association membership — IDEA, ERAI, ECIA, IPC, and SMTA membership can be declared and displayed on supplier profiles.
Complaint handling and enforcement

Any buyer can file a complaint against a listing or supplier using the report button on any search result. Complaints are reviewed by SupplyGrid and handled as follows.

Complaint review
All complaints are reviewed within 48 hours. Suppliers are not notified unless action is taken.
Warning and investigation
First or ambiguous complaints trigger an investigation. Suppliers may be contacted for documentation.
Listing removal
Confirmed inaccurate listings are removed immediately. Suppliers are notified with explanation.
Account termination
Three confirmed violations, or a single confirmed counterfeit complaint, results in permanent removal.
Protecting yourself as a buyer

SupplyGrid's trust signals are tools to assist your decision-making, not guarantees. We recommend the following practices when sourcing from any independent distributor or broker.

  • Prefer suppliers with Verified Business or Certified Supplier badges and established buyer ratings
  • For aerospace, defense, or medical applications, prioritize suppliers with AS9120, AS6081, or AS6171 certifications
  • Request a Certificate of Conformance and traceability documentation on all orders where application criticality warrants it
  • For large orders from unknown suppliers, consider having parts tested at an independent facility before acceptance
  • Report any discrepancy between what was listed and what was received using the report button on the listing
Reporting suspected counterfeits to authorities

If you receive parts you believe to be counterfeit, in addition to filing a complaint with SupplyGrid you should consider reporting to the relevant authorities in your jurisdiction.

  • USA — IPR Center (hsicrimes.dhs.gov), FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (ic3.gov), US Customs and Border Protection
  • Europe — EUIPO Observatory IP Enforcement Portal, relevant national customs authority
  • Industry — IDEA (idea-inc.org), ERAI (erai.com), GIDEP (gidep.org)

SupplyGrid cooperates with law enforcement investigations involving suspected counterfeit components distributed through the platform.

Report a supplier or listing

If you have experienced inaccurate stock, suspected counterfeits, or unresponsive suppliers on SupplyGrid, report it using the flag button on any search result. Reports are reviewed within 48 hours.
