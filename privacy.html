SUPPLYGRID

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy describes what information SupplyGrid collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We collect the minimum needed to run a component sourcing marketplace, and we do not sell personal information.

1. What we collect

Account information: email address, password (stored as a hash by our authentication provider), account type, name, phone if you provide it, company name, region, and country. Supplier content: listings, certifications, and verification documents you submit. Activity: part number searches, requests for quote you send or receive, want-to-buy requests, RFQ cart contents, reviews, disputes, and feature usage counts. Technical data: standard server logs, including IP addresses used for security and rate limiting.

2. How we use it

We use this information to operate the marketplace: showing listings to buyers, delivering RFQs to suppliers, displaying verification status and ratings, generating aggregate demand intelligence such as search counts, securing the platform, and improving the product. Demand intelligence shown to suppliers is aggregated and does not identify individual buyers.

3. What other users see

Supplier company profiles, including company name, region, country, verification status, ratings, and listings, are public. Supplier email addresses and phone numbers are not exposed publicly by the platform. When a buyer sends an RFQ, the buyer's provided contact details are shared with the receiving supplier so they can respond, which is the purpose of the platform. Anonymous listings hide the seller's identity from buyers, with inquiries routed through SupplyGrid.

4. Service providers

We use a small set of processors to run the platform: Supabase for database, authentication, and serverless functions, Vercel for website hosting, and Anthropic for AI processing of content you submit to AI features such as BOM parsing and listing enrichment. Content sent to AI features is used to provide the feature, not for advertising.

5. Cookies and tracking

We use only the storage needed to keep you signed in. We do not run advertising trackers and do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account from your dashboard settings. Deletion requests are processed by our team, removing your profile, listings, certifications, documents, and login. Some records may be retained where required for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

7. Security

Access to personal information is restricted with row-level security in our database, sensitive contact fields are not readable by other users, and administrative access is gated. No system is perfectly secure, so we encourage strong, unique passwords.

8. International users

The platform is operated from the United States and information is processed there. If you use SupplyGrid from other regions, you consent to this processing. Users in regions with data protection laws such as the GDPR may exercise access and deletion rights using the account deletion flow or by contacting us.

9. Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page. Questions and privacy requests can be sent through the contact options published on the platform.
